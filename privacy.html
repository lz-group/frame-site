FRAME: CAPTION LANE PRIVACY POLICY

Publisher: Lamonz International Productions, LLC · Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Updated: November 1, 2026 (added the website launch-notice signup)

The short version

FRAME: Caption Lane ("the app") does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers. Everything you make stays on your device.

This website has one optional, separate feature: a launch-notice signup, described below. It is not part of the app, and signing up does not change anything about the app.

Website launch-notice signup (separate from the app)

If you enter your email in the "Notify me" box on this website, we collect that email address and a note of which page you signed up from. We use it only to send you a notice when FRAME: Caption Lane is available on the App Store. We don't use it for anything else, we don't add it to other mailing lists, and we never sell or share it.

The signup is handled by Google Forms, and responses are stored in a Google Sheet in our company's Google Workspace account. Google processes the submission as our service provider under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your email at any time by writing to support+frame@lzgroupllc.com. The signup is not directed to children under 13. If a child under 13 has signed up, contact us and we will delete the email.

Your photos

The app uses the iOS system photo picker. It can only see the photo you choose, at the moment you choose it. That photo is edited on your device and is never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else.

When you tap Export, the app asks for permission to add images to your photo library and saves your finished image there. The app does not read or browse your library. You can change this permission anytime in Settings > Caption Lane > Photos.

Purchases

FRAME Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, and Apple processes it through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card number or any other payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether FRAME Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

What the app does not do

  • The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data.
  • The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, in the app or on this website.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included. For the website signup, see "Website launch-notice signup" above.

Apple

Apple may collect information on its own, such as App Store purchase records or crash reports you choose to share with developers. Apple's privacy policy covers that: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in the app or on this website.
