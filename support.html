What is FRAME: Caption Lane?

FRAME: Caption Lane turns your own photos into lock screens and posters. Pick a photo, choose a lane (a visual style) and a frame, add a caption, then export the result to Photos.

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it works offline.

What does FRAME Pro include?

FRAME Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes the "made with FRAME" watermark and unlocks every lane and frame.

I bought Pro on another device or reinstalled the app

Open Caption Lane and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Restoring is free.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every App Store payment and refund. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot see or refund payments ourselves.

Export says it was not saved

The app needs permission to add images to Photos. Go to Settings > Caption Lane > Photos and allow adding photos, then try again.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your device. The app never uploads your photos. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I set an image as my lock screen?

Open the exported image in Photos, tap Share, then choose Use as Wallpaper.
